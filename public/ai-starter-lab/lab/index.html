AI Starter Lab: your first week with AI, on your terms

A hands-on practice space for people who serve missions. Learn the house rules, build your first prompts, and try seven small exercises, one a day. No experience needed, and nothing to install.

Where this fits: Kate Gage's AI adoption life cycle, in AI for Nonprofits (Heyman and Contee, Wiley, 2025), moves through four stages. This lab is built for the first one.

Step 1

Five house rules before you type anything

Read them and check each one when you are ready to commit to it. These protect the people you serve and the trust you have earned.

    Does your organization have an AI policy? If not, start with the First AI Policy starter sheet you received with this webinar, and ask your leader which tools are approved for work.
    Step 2

    Starter prompts for your role

    Pick the role closest to yours. Copy a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot, then replace the highlighted parts with your own details. Leave out anything private.

    Step 3

    Build your own prompt

    Good prompts follow a simple recipe: who the AI should act as, what you need, the background it should know, and what the answer should look like. Fill in the boxes and your prompt builds itself below.

    A role or expertise
    One clear task
    Facts it needs. No personal or private details.
    Length and shape
    How it should sound
    Your prompt 
    
        
    
        
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    Step 4
    
    The seven-day practice challenge
    
    
    Ten to fifteen minutes a day. Each exercise uses low-risk, everyday work so you can learn what AI does well and where it falls short. Your check marks stay on this device only.
    
    
    
      
    
      
    
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
      
    Step 5
    
      Notice where the weight lands
      
    
      Readiness starts with honest noticing. At the end of your week, jot a few words for each question. Your notes stay on this device only.
